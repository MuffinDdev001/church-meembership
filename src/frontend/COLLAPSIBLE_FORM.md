# Collapsible Check Form - Completed ✅

## What Changed

The check generator form is now **collapsible** to give you more screen space!

## How to Use

### Hide the Form
1. Click the **"← Hide Form"** button at the top right
2. The entire left form panel disappears
3. Check preview expands to full width
4. More room to work on your check layout!

### Show the Form
1. Click **"→ Show Form"** button
2. Form panel reappears
3. Layout returns to side-by-side view

## Benefits

✅ **More screen space** for check layout  
✅ **Less scrolling** when working on design  
✅ **Cleaner interface** when you don't need the form  
✅ **Easier to see** whole check at once  
✅ **Toggle anytime** with one click  

## Form Features Preserved

When the form is visible, it now has:
- **Max height**: 85% of viewport
- **Scroll**: Only the form scrolls, not the whole page
- **All features**: Copy/paste, lines, resize, save layouts still work

## Perfect For

- **Layout Design**: Hide form, focus on positioning elements
- **Element Properties**: Form hidden, properties panel still visible on right
- **Printing**: Hide form, maximize check preview
- **Data Entry**: Show form, fill in check details

The form state is **NOT** saved, so it will show again when you refresh the page.
